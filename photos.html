the photo notice

We document our events. You can say no. Saying no costs you nothing and changes nothing about how welcome you are.

the short version

  • We take photos and short videos at hack//ops events.
  • They go on hackops.tech, LinkedIn, Instagram, event pages, and partner recaps for that operation.
  • On registration (lu.ma) you answer yes or no. We use that answer at check-in.
  • Want a picture taken down later? Write to team@hackops.tech.

what we shoot

Mostly the room and the work: benches, tools, screens, prototypes, demos, the podium. People show up in a lot of those frames while building.

We do not shoot your screen, notes, or badge on purpose. If something on your desk should stay private, say so and we drop the frame.

where it goes

  • hackops.tech — event and archive pages
  • LinkedIn and Instagram — recaps and announcements
  • Event pages (lu.ma) and material for the partners of that specific operation

Once a picture is on a social platform, that platform keeps it under its own terms. Tell us early if you want out.

saying no

Pick no on the lu.ma registration question. At check-in we give you a no-photo sticker or lanyard and brief whoever is shooting.

You can still change your mind on the day: tell any organiser, or the person with the camera, including mid shot. We note it.

Opting out covers pictures where you are recognisable. Wide room shots are harder; if you need to be out of those too, tell us and we work it out.

takedowns

Mail team@hackops.tech. Point at the picture or describe it. We remove it from everything we control. No reason needed.

the files

  • Originals stay in our storage, seen by the crew working on the recap.
  • We do not sell them or hand partners a photo library. Partner uses are picked and sent one by one.
  • No face recognition. No tagging without asking.
  • Published shots stay in that operation's archive until someone asks us to pull one.

who to ask

hack//ops, Karlsruhe. team@hackops.tech
