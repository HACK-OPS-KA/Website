the photo notice

We take pictures at our events, because a hackathon nobody documented might as well not have happened. Here is exactly what we shoot, where it ends up, and how to stay out of it. Saying no costs you nothing and changes nothing about how welcome you are.

the short version

  • Photos and short videos are taken at hack//ops events by our crew or by a photographer we brought.
  • They are used to document the event and to show what we do: this website, LinkedIn, Instagram, event pages, and recap posts.
  • You can say no. Tell any organiser, at any point, including after the fact.
  • Want a picture taken down? Write to team@hackops.tech and we remove it from everything we control.

what gets photographed

Mostly the room and the work: benches, soldering irons, screens, prototypes, demos, the podium during presentations. People appear in a lot of those pictures, usually while building something.

We do not photograph anyone's screen, notes, or badge deliberately. If your screen has something on it you would rather not see published, say so and we will re-shoot or drop the frame.

where it gets published

  • hackops.tech, on event and archive pages.
  • LinkedIn and Instagram, in recap and announcement posts.
  • Event pages such as lu.ma, and material we send to the partners of a specific operation.

Once a picture is on a social platform, that platform stores and processes it under its own terms, on servers that may be outside the EU. We cannot undo that part, which is one more reason to tell us early rather than late.

how to opt out

Three ways, all of them fine:

  • Tell an organiser when you arrive. We will note it and brief whoever is shooting.
  • Take a no-photo lanyard or sticker at check-in, if the operation has them, and we keep you out of frame.
  • Tell the person with the camera, at any moment, including mid shot.

Opting out applies to pictures where you are recognisable. Wide shots of a full room where you are one small figure among many are harder to police, so if you need to be out of those too, tell us and we will work it out with you.

taking something down

Write to team@hackops.tech. Point us at the picture, or just describe it. We take it off our site and our channels, and we do not ask for a reason.

what we do with the files

  • Originals live in our own storage and are seen by the crew working on the recap.
  • We do not sell them, and we do not hand them to sponsors or partners as a photo library. Pictures used in partner material are picked and sent one by one.
  • We do not run face recognition over them, and we do not tag people without asking.
  • Published pictures stay online as part of the archive of that operation unless somebody asks us to remove one.

who is responsible

hack//ops, Karlsruhe. Questions, objections and takedown requests all go to team@hackops.tech.

If you are in a picture, you have the right to ask what we hold, to have it corrected or deleted, and to object to us using it. Ask at the address above.

Draft, not legal advice. This page was written by the crew to be honest and readable, not by a lawyer. Before the next public push it should be checked by someone qualified, and hackops.tech still needs a proper Impressum and a general privacy policy alongside it.
