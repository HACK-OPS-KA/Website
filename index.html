How an op runs

01Sign up on Luma

Every op has its own Luma page. Sign-up, updates and the address are there.

02Come alone or with friends

Teams form at the op, on our platform. Nobody has to arrive with one.

03Build

Pick a track or a bounty, or build the thing you came for.

04Show it live

Teams demo on stage: a few minutes each, no slides.

05Keep it online

If your team agrees, the project goes into the showcase.

Building

How do I sign up?

On Luma. Each op has its own page; follow the HACK//OPS calendar there and you'll see every new op.

Do I need a team?

No. Teams form at the op on our platform. Come alone or bring friends.

Do I have to pitch?

No slides. Teams show what they built, live, in a few minutes.

Where can I see what people built?

In the showcase: every project its team chose to publish, op by op.

Companies

Can my company bring a challenge?

Yes. A challenge inside an op, a Hack Sprint for one evening, or a whole hackathon hosted with you. Write to us.

Do you share participants' data?

Only from people who opted in, and they can withdraw any time.
